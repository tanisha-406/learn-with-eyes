# 🌱 Learn with Eyes — Visual Learning Platform for Deaf Students

> **Empowering Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing Children Through Visual Education**  
> Developed for **Anushruti Academy for the Deaf (IIT Roorkee)** • Class 5 EVS / Science Curriculum  
> Textbook: *Innovators' Science 5* (EduRise India / NEP 2020, 120 Pages)  
> Full Textbook PDF: [`science-5-full.pdf`](./science-5-full.pdf) (55.99 MB, Complete 120 Pages)

---

## 📖 About the Project

**Anushruti Academy for the Deaf (AAD)**, situated within the campus of the **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee**, provides specialized education and vocational training to children with severe-to-profound hearing impairment.

Standard textbooks and digital educational platforms heavily rely on auditory lectures and dense blocks of text, creating significant learning barriers for deaf learners. **Learn with Eyes** is a dedicated, visual-first digital learning platform engineered specifically around deaf pedagogy:
- **Zero Auditory Dependence**: All conceptual explanations are communicated through diagrams, animations, color coding, and visual analogies.
- **Indian Sign Language (ISL) Vocabulary**: Interactive sign language cards with step-by-step gesture guides and handshape breakdowns for every chapter.
- **Interactive Tactile Activities**: Hands-on matching games designed for both desktop mice and classroom touch tablets (iPads, Android tablets).
- **NEP 2020 Interactive OMR Sheets**: Authentic bubble-filling assessment system for Model Test Papers with instant scoring and visual explanations.
- **Complete 120-Page Textbook PDF Integration**: Full offline-ready digital copy accessible directly from the app header and homepage.

## 📚 Complete Curriculum Coverage (All 14 Chapters & Model Test Papers)

The platform is mapped 1-to-1 with the complete 120-page textbook, with **all 14 chapters and test papers implemented as fully interactive modules**:

### 🌿 Unit I — Living Things and Their Needs (Pages 5–45)
1. **Chapter 1: Growing Plants (Page 5)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
   Seeds, germination stages, monocot vs. dicot, seed dispersal mechanisms, vegetative propagation, agriculture seasons.
2. **Chapter 2: Food and Health (Page 14)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
   5 essential nutrients (carbohydrates, fats, proteins, vitamins, minerals), balanced diet, deficiency diseases (night blindness, scurvy, rickets, goitre, anaemia), hygiene and communicable disease prevention.
3. **Chapter 3: Life of Animals (Page 23)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
   Terrestrial, aquatic, amphibious, arboreal, and aerial habitats; 4 breathing organs (gills, spiracles, moist skin, lungs); movement adaptations; long-distance seasonal animal migration (Arctic Tern, Monarch Butterfly, Siberian Crane).
4. **Chapter 4: The Skeletal and Muscular System (Page 31)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
   Human skeleton (206 bones, skull with 22 bones, spine with 33 vertebrae, ribcage with 12 pairs); 4 moveable joints (ball & socket, hinge, pivot, gliding); voluntary, involuntary, and cardiac muscles.
5. **Chapter 5: Nervous System (Page 40)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
   Central nervous system: Brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, medulla oblongata), spinal cord involuntary reflex arc, sensory/motor/mixed nerves, 5 sense organs (eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin).

### ⚡ Unit II — Materials and Energy (Pages 46–70)
6. **Chapter 6: Solids, Liquids and Gases (Page 46)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
   Kinetic molecular theory, arrangements in 3 states of matter, solutions (solute, solvent, solution), miscible vs. immiscible liquids, physical vs. chemical changes.
7. **Chapter 7: Force, Energy and Simple Machines (Page 52)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
   Push vs. pull, magnetic, gravitational, frictional, buoyant, and elastic forces; scientific definition of work; 6 forms of energy; 6 simple machines (lever, pulley, wheel & axle, inclined plane, wedge, screw).
8. **Chapter 8: Air and Water (Page 61)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
   5 atmospheric layers (troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere, thermosphere, exosphere); air composition (78% N₂, 21% O₂, 0.04% CO₂); water purification stages (sedimentation + alum loading, decantation, filtration, chlorination, distillation).

### 🌍 Unit III — Earth and Environment (Pages 71–112)
9. **Chapter 9: Soil Erosion and Conservation (Page 71)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
   Soil profile (topsoil with humus, subsoil, bedrock); erosion agents (water, wind, deforestation, overgrazing); soil conservation techniques (afforestation, step/terrace farming, bund construction, cover crops).
10. **Chapter 10: Rocks and Minerals (Page 77)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
    Igneous rocks (granite, pumice, obsidian, basalt); sedimentary rocks (sandstone, limestone, shale, conglomerate); metamorphic rocks (marble, slate, quartzite, gneiss); mineral classification (metallic vs. non-metallic); fossil fuel conservation.
11. **Chapter 11: The Earth and The Solar System (Page 87)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
    The Sun; 8 planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune); Earth's Moon (vacuum, craters, phases); Solar eclipse vs. Lunar eclipse; Artificial satellites (weather, telecommunications, GPS navigation).
12. **Chapter 12: Natural Disasters (Page 97)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
    Earthquakes (focus, epicentre, seismograph, Richter scale, Drop-Cover-Hold drill); Volcanoes (magma vs. lava, active, dormant, extinct); Floods, droughts, and tsunamis; Emergency safety rules.
13. **Chapter 13: Our Environment (Page 105)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
    Ecosystem balance: Biotic vs. Abiotic components; Producers, consumers, decomposers, and food chains; 4 types of pollution (air, water, soil, noise); Waste segregation (biodegradable vs. non-biodegradable) & the 3Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle).

### 🩹 Unit IV — Safety & Assessments (Pages 113–120)
14. **Chapter 14: Safety Rules and First Aid (Page 113)** `[Interactive Module ✨]`  
    Road safety (pedestrian crossings, traffic signals); Fire safety (Fire Triangle: Fuel, Heat, Oxygen; fire extinguisher operation); First aid treatment for minor wounds, burns, sprains, fractures, and animal bites.
- **Model Test Paper 1 (Page 119)** `[Interactive OMR Assessment 📝]`  
  Comprehensive mid-term evaluation covering Chapters 1–7 with interactive NEP 2020 OMR bubble sheet, automated scoring, and question-by-question visual explanations.
- **Model Test Paper 2 (Page 120)** `[Interactive OMR Assessment 📝]`  
  Final evaluation covering Chapters 8–14 with authentic OMR bubble filling, answer sheet validation, score badges, and detailed review.

## 🚀 Key Features & Accessibility Innovations

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **📘 120-Page Full Textbook PDF** | Complete high-resolution digital compilation of *Innovators' Science 5* (EduRise India) available for 1-click download and offline reading. |
| **🤟 Accessible ISL Modal System** | Interactive modal dialogs featuring animated handshape guides, step-by-step gesture steps, and scientific definitions across curriculum vocabulary signs. |
| **🎮 Universal Tactile Matching Games** | Works with **both** desktop mice AND **Touch/Click-to-Match** for classroom touchscreens. Includes real-time score tracking, reset buttons, and celebratory feedback. |
| **📝 Interactive NEP 2020 OMR Sheets** | Authentic bubble-filling interface simulating standard examination answer sheets for Model Test Papers 1 & 2 with real-time feedback and review. |
| **💡 Visual Quizzes with Explanations** | All 14 chapters feature interactive multiple-choice visual assessments with instant color feedback, comprehensive explanations, star ratings (🌟🌟🌟), and retry functionality. |
| **🧭 Curriculum Filtering & Navigation** | One-click filter chips to view by unit (`All`, `Unit I`, `Unit II`, `Unit III`, `Unit IV`) or jump straight to any chapter from the sidebar or home grid. |
| **🌙 Dark & High-Contrast Modes** | Full theme switcher (Light / Dark / High-Contrast) designed to minimize visual fatigue for low-vision and deaf learners. |
| **🔤 Dynamic Text Scaling** | One-click font sizing (`Normal` → `Large` → `Extra Large`) for accessibility across varying screen distances. |
| **💾 Progress Persistence** | Uses `localStorage` to preserve quiz completions, star rewards, theme preferences, and font scaling across browser restarts. |
| **📱 100% Offline Capable** | Built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript with no npm dependencies, ensuring instant offline classroom usability. |

## 🛠️ How to Run Locally

You don't need any complex build steps or dependencies:

### Method 1: Direct Browser
Simply double-click `index.html` or open it directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

### Method 2: Local HTTP Server (Recommended)
Using Python:
```bash
python -m http.server 3000
```
Then open: [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)
